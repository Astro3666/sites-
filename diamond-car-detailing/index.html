The diamond process

Four stages to a mirror finish

Like cutting a stone, the shine comes from patient work in the right order. Every full detail follows the same four stages.

01

Wash

Snow foam and a gentle hand wash lift the sand and dust without dragging it across the paint.

02

Decontaminate

Iron remover and clay bar pull out what washing leaves behind, so the surface is smooth as glass.

03

Correct

Machine polishing levels the clear coat and removes swirls, so reflections turn sharp again.

04

Protect

Ceramic coating, sealant or wax locks in the gloss and keeps sun, dust and water off the paint.

Questions

Good to know

What is the difference between polishing and ceramic coating?

Polishing corrects the paint: it removes swirls and scratches and brings back the shine. Ceramic coating protects it: it locks that shine in and shields the paint from sun, dust and water. For the best result we polish first and coat after.

How long does a detail take?

A wash and wax is quick, while paint correction and ceramic coating can take a full day or more, depending on the car and its condition. We give you a time when you book.

Can every scratch be removed?

Swirls and light scratches usually polish out completely. Deep scratches that go through the clear coat can be improved but may need paint work. Send us a photo and we will tell you honestly what to expect.

How do I look after the car afterwards?

Hand wash with a pH-neutral shampoo and a clean mitt, avoid brush car washes and dry with a soft microfibre towel. We give you simple aftercare tips with every coating.
