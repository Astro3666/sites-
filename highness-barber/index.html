Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. If you want a set time, especially in the evening, send a WhatsApp message and we will hold a chair for you.

Where exactly are you?

At Al Ghubra Roundabout in Muscat. Tap Google Maps or Waze on this page and it will take you straight to the door.

How late are you open?

Until midnight, Saturday to Thursday, from 10 in the morning. Message us for Friday hours.

What is a Turkish shave?

A slow, close shave with hot towels, warm lather and a straight razor, finished with a cold towel and cologne. Tell your barber if your skin is sensitive to fragrance.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes, kids are welcome. Quieter daytime hours are the easiest for little ones.
