How we work

Four steps to a mirror

Polishing a dirty car only grinds the dirt in. So the order matters: clean first, look closely, then polish, then protect.

1.

Wash & decontaminate

Foam, hand wash and clay take off dust, tar and the grit you cannot see.

2.

Inspect under light

A strong light shows every swirl and scratch, so we choose the right pad and polish.

3.

Machine polish

Panel by panel, the polisher levels the clear coat until the reflection is sharp.

4.

Protect

Wax, sealant or ceramic goes on last, so the gloss lasts and water beads off.

Questions

Good to know

Will polishing remove every scratch?

Polishing removes swirls, haze and scratches that sit in the clear coat. A scratch you can catch with a fingernail may be too deep to polish out fully, but it usually becomes much harder to see. We will tell you honestly before we start.

What is the difference between polish, wax and ceramic?

Polish fixes the paint by levelling out defects. Wax and ceramic do not fix anything; they protect the paint after it is polished. Wax is quick and gives a warm shine, ceramic is harder and lasts much longer.

How long does it take?

A wash and wax is quick. A machine polish takes most of a day, and a full correction with ceramic can take longer, depending on the car. We will give you a time when you book.

Is polishing safe for my paint?

Yes, when it is done with care. We check the paint first and only take off as little clear coat as needed to get the defects out.
