Questions

Good to know

Is L’Artista for ladies only?

Yes. L’Artista is a lady center: a private, comfortable space for women.

How do I book?

Use the booking form on this page or message us on WhatsApp at +968 9575 6198. We reply to confirm your time.

Can I just walk in?

Send us a quick message before you come and we will tell you when a chair is free, so you don’t have to wait.

What are your prices?

Prices depend on the service and on your hair. Ask us on WhatsApp and we will send you the current menu.

When are you open?

Monday to Saturday, 10:15 am to 8:00 pm. We are closed on Sunday.
