How we wash

Four steps, no scratches

Most swirl marks come from rubbing grit across the paint. Our order of work is built to get the grit off before anything touches the car.

1

Pre-rinse

Low-pressure water knocks off loose sand and dust from the roof down.

2

Snow foam

Thick foam clings to every panel and softens the dirt so it slides off with the rinse.

3

Hand wash

Soft mitts and two buckets, one for soapy water and one to rinse the mitt clean.

4

Dry & finish

Plush towels, tyre shine and glass wiped for a clean, streak-free finish.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to book?

Booking on WhatsApp is the easiest way to get a time that suits you, especially for interior work and full details. Message us and we will reply with a slot.

How long does it take?

An express wash is quick. A full wash takes longer because of the interior, and a showroom detail with polishing can take most of a day. We will give you a time when you book.

Is it safe for ceramic coatings and wraps?

Yes. Foam and a gentle hand wash are the recommended way to clean coated, wrapped and PPF-protected cars. Tell us when you book and we will skip anything that could harm the coating.

Why does a hand wash look better than an automatic one?

Brushes in automatic washes drag grit from car to car across your paint, which leaves fine circular scratches. Washing by hand with clean mitts keeps the finish deep and glossy.
