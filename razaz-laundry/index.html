How it works

Five stops along the rail

Every piece moves through the same five stations, in this order, before it comes back to you.

Station 1

Hand it over

Drop your clothes at the shop or message us with what you have. Confirm pickup & delivery

Station 2

Check & sort

Pockets emptied, labels read, stains noted and pointed out to you before cleaning.

Station 3

Clean

Washed or dry-cleaned by fabric and colour, with stain treatment where it is needed.

Station 4

Press

Steam-pressed, collars and cuffs set, then hung or folded the way the piece is worn.

Station 5

Ready

Bagged and ready to collect. We message you when your order is done.

Care labels
 
Read the label like we do
 
Every piece has a care label sewn inside. These are the symbols we read before we clean anything.
 
 
 
Machine wash at 30°
The number is the hottest water the fabric can take.
 
Hand wash
Gentle washing by hand in cool water. No wringing.
 
Do not bleach
No bleach of any kind, or the colour will be damaged.
 
Tumble dry, low heat
One dot means low heat. Two dots means normal heat.
 
Iron, medium heat
Dots show the heat: one for silk and synthetics, three for cotton and linen.
 
Do not iron
Steam only, from a distance, or no heat at all.
 
Dry clean
A circle means dry clean. The letter tells the cleaner which solvent to use.
 
Do not dry clean
Water washing only. Tell us if you see this on a delicate piece.

Questions
 
Good to know
 
 
 How long will my order take? 
It depends on the service and how many pieces you bring. Ask when you drop off and we will give you a ready time. Add your usual turnaround
 
 
 Can you get this stain out? 
Tell us what the stain is and how long it has been there. Fresh stains come out far more easily, so bring them in soon and don’t rub them. Some old oil, ink or dye stains may not fully come out, and we will tell you before we start.
 
 
 Do you collect and deliver? 
Add whether you offer pickup and delivery, and which areas
 
 
 What should I check before handing over clothes? 
Empty the pockets, and point out any stains, loose buttons or pieces that need special care. If something is valuable or delicate, tell us so we can treat it on its own.
