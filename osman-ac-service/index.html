Inside your AC

Four parts a deep clean reaches

A quick rinse only touches the filters. The dust that costs you cooling sits deeper, so we take the unit apart to reach it.

01

Air filters

The first line against dust. Clogged filters choke the airflow and make the unit work harder.

02

Cooling coil

Where the air gets cold. A dusty coil cools less, uses more power and can start to smell.

03

Blower wheel

It pushes the air into your room. Dust on its blades weakens the airflow and makes it noisy.

04

Drain pan

It catches the water the coil pulls out of the air. When it blocks, water runs down your wall.

Questions

Good to know

How often should I service my AC in Oman?

A deep clean twice a year, before and after the summer, is a good rule. In the hottest months and dusty weeks, wash the filters every month.

How long does a deep clean take?

It depends on the type of unit, where it is mounted and how dirty it is. We tell you before we start.

Will my room get wet or dirty?

No. The unit is covered while it is washed, the floor and furniture are protected, and we clean up before we leave.

Do you work with offices and shops?

Yes, homes, villas, offices and shops. Ask us about regular visits if you have several units.

Which areas do you cover?

We work in Muscat. Send us your area and we will confirm a visit.
