Process

Four stages, always in order

Polishing a dirty car only grinds the dirt in. Skipping a stage shows later, so we never do.

  1. Wash & decontaminate

    Foam, hand wash, iron remover and clay leave the surface truly clean, so nothing gets dragged around in the next steps.

  2. Inspect under light

    Paint, lenses and trim are checked under strong lights. We tell you what we see and what can be fixed before any machine touches the car.

  3. Correct

    Sanding and machine polishing level out swirls, oxidation and haze until reflections are sharp and lenses are clear.

  4. Protect

    A sealant, UV lens coat or ceramic coating locks the result in against the Omani sun.

Questions

Good to know

Why do headlights turn yellow?

Headlight lenses are plastic with a thin factory UV coat. Years of strong sun break that coat down, and the plastic underneath oxidises and goes cloudy. Restoring it means removing the damaged layer and sealing the lens again, not just polishing the surface.

What is paint correction?

Machine polishing that removes a very thin layer of clear coat to level out swirls, light scratches and water spots, so the paint reflects clearly instead of looking hazy.

How long does a ceramic coating last?

It depends on the coating and how the car is washed afterwards. We tell you which product we use and what to expect from it when we quote, along with simple aftercare advice.

How long do you need the car?

A maintenance detail is done the same day. Correction and coating take longer, because each stage needs its time and coatings have to cure. We give you a time when you book.
