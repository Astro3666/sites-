    The craft

    Scissors over comb

    The comb lifts each section of hair to an even height and the scissors take off only what sits above it. It is slower than clippers alone, and it is how a longer top keeps its shape as it grows.

    • Clippers for the sides, scissors for the top
    • Edges lined up around the ears and neck
    • A last check in the mirror before you go
