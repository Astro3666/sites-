Questions

Good to know

How often should I do pest control in Muscat?

In Oman's heat many homes book a treatment every few months, more often in summer. After the first visit we will tell you what your home needs.

Do we need to leave the house during treatment?

It depends on the treatment. Before we start, we tell you which rooms to keep closed and for how long, especially for children and pets.

Can you clean and do pest control in the same visit?

Yes. Tell us both when you book and we will plan the visit so the cleaning and the treatment work together.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the home and the problem. Send a photo and a short description on WhatsApp and we will reply with a price.
