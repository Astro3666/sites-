Questions

Good to know

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

No. We arrive with our own water, power and products. All we need is access to the car.

How long does a wash take?

It depends on the package and the size of the car. An exterior wash is quick, while a full detail takes longer. We tell you the time when you book.

Can you wash my car while I am at work?

Yes, as long as we can reach the car and the building allows it. Leave us the key or keep it with you for an exterior-only wash.

Is it safe to wash in the summer heat?

We work in shade where we can and keep panels wet until they are dried by hand, so water spots never bake onto the paint.
