Before you order

Good to know

How far ahead should I order?

A single box can often be ready the same day. For favours, trays and large orders, message us as early as you can so we can plan the quantities.

Can you add names and dates?

Yes. Send us the names, the date and the colours of the event, and we suggest wrapping and tags to match.

Do you deliver?

Ask us on WhatsApp with your area and the date, and we tell you what is possible. You can always collect from the shop.

How should I keep the chocolates?

Somewhere cool and dry, out of the sun, and never in a parked car. Muscat afternoons are hard on chocolate.
