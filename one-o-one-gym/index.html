FAQ

Good questions.

Is the gym really women-only?

Yes. One O One is a ladies gym, so the members you train alongside are all women.

I have never trained before. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Most people start as beginners. We will show you how the equipment works and help you build a routine you can stick to.

What are your opening hours?

Saturday to Thursday, 9:00 am to 10:30 pm. Message us to check Friday and holiday hours.

Can I visit before I join?

Of course. Send us a WhatsApp message and drop by for a look around before you choose a plan.

Where exactly are you?

On Al Maha Street in Bawshar, Muscat. Use the Google Maps or Waze buttons below for turn-by-turn directions.
