How to order

From branch to their door

  1. Choose

    Build your bouquet above, or send us a photo of something you love.

  2. Confirm

    We reply on WhatsApp with the price and the time, before anything is cut.

  3. Receive

    Your flowers are made fresh for the day you chose, with your card tucked in.

Questions

Do you deliver?

Tell us your area and the time you need the flowers on WhatsApp, and we will let you know the delivery options and charge.

How early should I order?

For bouquets, ordering a day ahead gives you the most choice. For weddings and events, contact us as early as you can.

Can I choose the flowers and colours?

Yes. Pick a palette in the builder or describe what you want, and you are welcome to send a reference photo.

Can you add a card?

Yes. Write your message in the builder, in Arabic or English, and it goes with the flowers.

How do I pay?

We confirm the price on WhatsApp before we start, and share the payment options at the same time.
