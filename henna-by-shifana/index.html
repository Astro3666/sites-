    After your henna

    Let the colour come up

    Natural henna never looks finished on day one. It starts bright orange and darkens for about two days. Drag the slider to see how.

    1. Keep the paste on

      Leave it as long as you comfortably can, ideally for several hours or overnight. Warmth helps the colour.

    2. Scrape it off, don't wash it

      Flake the dry paste away with your fingers or the back of a spoon. Try to keep water away for the next 12 hours.

    3. Let it deepen

      The orange turns reddish brown over the first day and reaches its deepest colour around day two.

    4. Make it last

      A little natural oil or balm before washing helps. Palms and fingertips hold the colour longest.

    Questions

    Before your henna

    How early should I book for a wedding?

    As early as you know your date. Wedding season and the days before Eid fill up first, and a bridal design takes time to plan.

    How long does the henna take?

    A simple design can take ten to twenty minutes. A full bridal look on hands and feet can take several hours, so plan a relaxed afternoon or evening.

    How long will it last?

    Usually one to three weeks. It fades gently as your skin renews, fastest on the backs of hands and slowest on palms and fingertips.

    Why is my stain orange at first?

    That is how natural henna works. It starts orange when the paste comes off and darkens to reddish brown over about two days. Henna that goes black straight away is not natural henna and can be harmful.
