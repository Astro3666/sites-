A filter rinse isn't a clean

The filter catches the big dust. The fine dust and damp get past it and settle on the coil, the blower and the tray. That's where the smell, the drips and the weak cooling come from.

Why it matters here

Oman is hard on air conditioners

Months of non-stop running

Muscat summers pass 45°C, so most units run all day for half the year. Dirt builds up faster than you'd think.

Fine dust and sand

It slips past the filter and sticks to the wet coil, where it turns into a grey felt that blocks the air.

Coastal humidity

Damp air keeps the inside of the unit wet, which is exactly what mould on the blower wheel needs.

How often should a split AC be washed?

In Oman's dust, twice a year: once before summer and once after. Rinse the filters every few weeks in between.

Do you take the unit off the wall?

No. It's washed where it hangs, inside a cover bag. Only the panel, filters and louver come off.

Will water get on my wall or floor?

The cover bag catches the water and runs it into a bucket, and we cover the floor and furniture under the unit.

How long does one unit take?

Usually around an hour, a bit longer if it hasn't been washed in years. You get a time when you book.
