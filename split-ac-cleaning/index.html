Inside your AC

The dirt you can't see is the dirt that matters

A split unit has five parts that trap dust and damp. A filter rinse only reaches the first two. Tap a part to see what goes wrong and what we do about it.

  1. 1

    Front panel & louver

    Greasy film and dust that blow straight back into the room. We hand-wash and dry them.

  2. 2

    Air filters

    Packed with dust, so less air gets through. Washed, dried and refitted.

  3. 3

    Cooling coil

    Dust sticks to the wet fins and blocks cooling. Foam cleaner, then a pressure rinse.

  4. 4

    Blower wheel

    Black mould on the blades makes the smell and the noise. Scrubbed and rinsed blade by blade.

  5. 5

    Drain tray & pipe

    Sludge here is why water drips down the wall. Flushed until it runs clear.

How often should I clean my split AC?

In Oman's dust we suggest a deep clean twice a year, once before summer and once after. Wash the filters every few weeks in between, or ask us to.

Do you take the unit off the wall?

No. We fit a cover bag around it and wash it where it hangs. The panel, filters and louver come off; the coil, blower and tray are washed in place.

Will it make a mess?

The bag catches the water and runs it into a bucket. We cover furniture and the floor under the unit, and leave the room as we found it.

How long does it take?

Usually around an hour for one split unit, a little longer if it hasn't been cleaned in years. We'll give you a time when you book.
