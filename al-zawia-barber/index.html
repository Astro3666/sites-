The Turkish ritual

Four steps, no rush

The shave Turkish barbers are known for, the same one playing at the top of this page.

1

Hot towel

A steamed towel softens the beard and opens the skin, so the blade glides instead of pulling.

2

Lather

Shaving soap is whipped in a copper bowl with a brush, then worked into the beard in circles.

3

The blade

A straight razor with a fresh blade, in two passes: first with the grain, then a closer finish.

4

Flame and cologne

A quick pass of flame tidies fine hair on the ears, and a splash of cologne cools the skin.

Ask the barber which steps your service includes. You can skip the flame.

Questions

Before you come in

Do I need to book?
Booking holds your time. If you want to come now, message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you if a chair is free.
When are you open?
Saturday to Thursday from 12 noon to midnight, and Friday from 4pm to midnight.
What is the flame for?
It is a quick, traditional pass of flame that singes away fine hair on the ears. It takes seconds and you can always ask to skip it.
Where exactly is the shop?
In Ruwi, Muscat 112. Send us a message on WhatsApp and we will reply with a location pin.
