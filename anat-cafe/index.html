The gate

A cafe inside Nizwa's history

Harat Al Aqr is one of the oldest quarters of Nizwa, beside the fort and the souq. At its entrance stands Bab Sabah Abi Moather, a gate thought to be more than 1,200 years old.

The cafe came out of a heritage inn project that restores the quarter's old houses. The gate's wooden door was restored, and so was a room beside it where grain was once ground by millstone. Today young Omanis make the coffee here and share the story of Nizwa and the quarter with visitors.

Source: Times of Oman

Walking in

Finding the gate

The quarter is a web of narrow lanes that cars don't fit easily. This is how to reach the cafe in Nizwa.

  1. Park by the souq

    There's little parking by the quarter. The Nizwa Souq and fort car parks are closest.

  2. Walk into Harat Al Aqr

    Head into the old quarter behind the souq, between the restored mud houses.

  3. Look for the gate

    Bab Sabah Abi Moather is the old gate with the wooden door. The cafe is inside it.

  4. Find a seat

    Order your coffee, then sit between the mud walls or ask about the roof terrace.
