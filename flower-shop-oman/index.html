Questions

Good to know

Do you deliver?

Yes, across Muscat. Tell us the area and the time you need it, and we will confirm the delivery charge on WhatsApp.

Can I order for today?

Message us as early in the day as you can. Same-day orders depend on the flowers we have in the shop that morning.

Can you match a photo or a colour?

Yes. Send us a picture, a colour or even an outfit, and we will build the arrangement around it.

How do I make my flowers last longer?

Trim the stems at an angle, change the water every two days and keep the vase away from direct sun and air-conditioning vents. Most cut flowers last five to seven days.
