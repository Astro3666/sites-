The house

Built like an old Omani home

Kahwa ritual

How kahwa is poured

In Oman, coffee has its manners. If it's your first visit, this is what happens at your table.

  1. Dates first

    A plate of dates arrives before the coffee. Their sweetness balances the bitter kahwa.

  2. A small pour

    Only a little goes into the finjan, so every sip stays hot.

  3. Right hand

    Take the cup and drink with your right hand.

  4. Shake to finish

    When you've had enough, give the cup a gentle shake. Until then, it keeps being refilled.
