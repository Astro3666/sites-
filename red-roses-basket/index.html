Questions

Good to know

Do you deliver?

Yes, across Muscat. Tell us the area and the time you need it, and we will confirm the delivery charge on WhatsApp.

Can you deliver at an exact time, like a surprise?

Tell us the moment you have in mind and we will plan the delivery around it, and confirm the time with you before we set out.

Can I order for today?

Message us as early in the day as you can. Same-day baskets depend on the roses we have that morning, especially for 50 or 100 roses.

How do I keep the roses fresh?

Keep the basket away from direct sun and air-conditioning vents, and add a little water to the floral foam every day. Most roses stay beautiful for five to seven days.
