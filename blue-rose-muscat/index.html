Questions

Before you order

Are blue roses real roses?

Yes. Blue roses don't grow in nature, so they start as fresh white roses and are coloured blue. They are real flowers and need fresh water like any rose.

How long will they last?

Like any fresh rose, it depends on care. Keep them out of direct sun and away from air-con vents. We'll share care tips with your order.

Do you deliver, and where?

Tell us your area when you order and we'll confirm delivery and timing. Delivery areas to confirm

Can I order for today?

Message us as early as you can and we'll tell you what's possible for the same day. Same-day policy to confirm

Can you match a colour or theme?

Send us a photo of the colour, dress or décor and we'll suggest the closest shades and flowers.
