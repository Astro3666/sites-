Pest guide

What are you seeing?

Pick a pest to see the signs, where it hides and how we deal with it.

Cockroaches

Signs

Seen at night near sinks and drains, small dark droppings, a musty smell in cupboards.

Where they hide

Behind kitchen cabinets, under fridges and cookers, in drains and bathroom ducts.

How we treat

Gel bait in cracks and hinges, treatment along drains and skirting, then a follow-up to catch the next hatch.

Ask about this pest

Termites

Signs

Mud tubes on walls or door frames, wood that sounds hollow, small wings left near windows.

Where they hide

Inside door and window frames, wooden furniture and the soil under the floor slab.

How we treat

An inspection to trace their path, then treatment of the soil and affected wood, with baiting for active colonies.

Ask about this pest

Bed bugs

Signs

Itchy bites in a line, small blood spots on sheets, dark specks along mattress seams.

Where they hide

Mattress seams, bed frames and headboards, sofa joints and the edges of carpets.

How we treat

Room by room treatment of beds and furniture, and a second visit to catch the eggs that hatch afterwards.

Ask about this pest

Rats & mice

Signs

Droppings, chewed food packets or cables, scratching in the ceiling at night.

Where they hide

Roof spaces, store rooms, gardens and behind kitchen appliances.

How we treat

Locked bait stations and traps placed on their routes, and we point out the gaps to close so they cannot return.

Ask about this pest

Mosquitoes

Signs

Bites at dusk, buzzing at night, swarms around plants and water.

Where they breed

Standing water: tanks, AC drip trays, plant saucers, drains and garden fountains.

How we treat

Larvicide where they breed, residual spray where they rest, and fogging for gardens and courtyards.

Ask about this pest

Ants

Signs

Trails to sugar and food, small piles of soil next to tiles and skirting.

Where they hide

Cracks in walls, under floor tiles and in garden beds close to the house.

How we treat

Bait on the trail so workers carry it back to the nest, then a barrier along the entry points.

Safety

Safe for your family

A few simple steps before and after the visit keep children, pets and food safe and help the treatment work.

Before we arrive

  • Clear kitchen counters and put food away in closed containers.
  • Move pets, birds and fish tanks out of the rooms we treat.
  • Tell us if anyone at home is pregnant, or has asthma or allergies.

After the treatment

  • Stay out of sprayed rooms until surfaces are dry. We tell you how long.
  • Don't mop the treated cracks and skirting for a few days.
  • Seeing a few pests for some days is normal while the treatment works.

Questions

Good to know

Is the treatment safe for children and pets?

We use targeted treatments and tell you exactly which rooms to keep clear and for how long. Let us know when you book if anyone at home has asthma or allergies.

Do I have to leave the house?

Usually not for gel and bait treatments. For sprays you stay out of the treated rooms until they dry. We tell you before the visit.

How many visits will I need?

Ants and general insects often need one visit. Bed bugs, cockroaches and termites usually need a follow-up, because eggs hatch after the first treatment.

Do you work with restaurants and offices?

Yes. We treat commercial kitchens, offices, shops and buildings, and can set up scheduled visits.
