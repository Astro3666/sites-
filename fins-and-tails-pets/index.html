Your first aquarium

Four steps to a tank that thrives

Most first-tank problems come from rushing. This is the order we walk every new fish keeper through.

1

Pick the tank

Bigger is easier: more water means steadier temperature and cleaner water. Choose the spot first, away from direct sun.

2

Set up & cycle

Add gravel, plants, filter and heater, treat the tap water, and let the filter run so good bacteria can build up.

Usually a few weeks before fish go in
3

Add fish slowly

Start with a few hardy fish. Float the bag so the temperature matches, then let them out gently.

Float the bag 15 to 20 minutes
4

Keep a routine

Feed only what they finish in a couple of minutes and change part of the water every week.

Around 10 to 25% of the water weekly

Pet care

Questions we hear every day

How much should I feed my fish?

Once or twice a day, only as much as they finish in about two minutes. Leftover food is the most common reason for cloudy water.

Can a goldfish live in a bowl?

Goldfish grow larger and produce more waste than most people expect. They are much healthier in a filtered tank with plenty of water.

Why is my new tank cloudy?

A milky haze in the first weeks is usually the tank settling in while bacteria build up in the filter. Feed lightly and avoid big changes. If fish look stressed, bring us a water sample and we will take a look.

How do I switch my cat or dog to a new food?

Mix the new food in gradually over about a week, a little more each day, so their stomach has time to adjust.
