    Questions

    Before you come in

    When are you open?

    Every day from 10am to 11pm. On Saturdays the doors open earlier, at 8am.

    Do I need to book?

    Booking holds a time for you. If you want to come now, send a WhatsApp message first and the shop will tell you if a chair is free.

    I don't know what cut I want. What should I do?

    Bring a photo of a haircut you like. It is the quickest way to agree on the length, the fade and the shape.

    How do I find the shop?

    Search the plus code H92X+898 in Google Maps, or tap Open in Google Maps above. You can also ask for directions on WhatsApp.

    How much is a haircut?

    Send a message on WhatsApp and the shop will tell you today's price for the cut you want.

    Can I ask for the same barber next time?

    Yes. Mention who cut your hair when you message, and the shop will try to book you with them.
