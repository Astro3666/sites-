    Questions

    Before you come in

    Do I need to book?

    Booking holds your time with your barber. If you want to come now, message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you if a chair is free.

    Do you cut children's hair?

    Yes. A haircut for children under 10 is 3 OMR and takes about 30 minutes.

    How long does keratin take?

    About 90 minutes for short hair and around two hours for medium or long hair. Prices are 20, 35 and 50 OMR by length.

    How can I pay?

    Cash or card.

    What if I am running late?

    Message us as soon as you know. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late we may need to move your booking, and missed or late-cancelled bookings can be charged in full. Cancel at least 24 hours ahead to avoid that.
