Questions

Good to know

How long does it take?

It depends on the service and how many pieces you bring. Tell us when you drop off and we give you a ready time. If you need something urgently, message us first.

Do you take dishdashas and abayas?

Yes. Dishdashas are washed to keep them bright and pressed with a clean collar and a straight front line. Abayas are washed gently and finished on low steam.

Can you remove stains?

Show us the stain when you drop off and tell us what it is, and we treat it before washing. Some old or set-in stains cannot be fully removed, and we will tell you honestly before we start.
