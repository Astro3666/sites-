The window table

The same seat, a different view

Al Baleed Beach changes through the day and through the year. Here is when to come.

Before 11 am

Morning light

Calm water, soft sun through the big windows and the pick of the tables.

June to September

Khareef season

Mist on the sea, green hills behind and a hot coffee in the cool air.

Afternoon

Cake o'clock

A slice and a caramel latte while the sun drops toward the water.

Late night

After the beach

Waves in the dark, a breeze on the outdoor seats and dessert to finish the day.
