Services

From the dishdasha you wear every day to the suit you wear twice a year. We check each label first and choose the right wash, temperature and press.

How it works

How your order moves through the shop

01

Hand it in

Bring your clothes to the counter, or send the list on WhatsApp first so we can tell you the price and the ready date.

02

Tagged & sorted

Each piece gets your ticket number and is sorted by fabric and colour. Stains are pointed out and treated first.

03

Cleaned & steamed

Washed or dry cleaned to the label, then steam pressed by hand so collars, cuffs and lines sit right.

04

Collect, ready to wear

Show your ticket and take everything home on hangers, bagged against the dust, or folded if you prefer.

Questions

Good to know

When will my clothes be ready?

It depends on the items and how busy the week is. We write the ready date on your ticket when you hand the clothes in, and you can ask on WhatsApp before you come.

Can you clean an abaya with stones or embroidery?

Yes. Tell us at the counter and we check the stones, beads and thread first, then choose a gentle wash or dry cleaning so nothing loosens or fades.

Will the stain come out?

We treat stains before cleaning. Fresh stains usually come out. Old stains or ones that went through a hot dryer may only fade, and we tell you that before we start.

Do you collect and deliver?

Send us a message with your area and we will tell you what is possible. Confirm pickup and delivery

Can I have my dishdasha starched?

Yes. Ask for light, medium or firm starch when you hand it in and we note it on your ticket.
