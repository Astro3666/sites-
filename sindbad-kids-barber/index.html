The voyage

A visit, in five steps

Like every good voyage, a haircut at Sindbad has a beginning, a middle and a happy ending.

  1. 1

    Welcome aboard

    Your child gets a few minutes to look around and climb into the chair.

  2. 2

    Pick the look

    Show us a photo or tell us the style. We agree it with you before we start.

  3. 3

    The cut

    Scissors and clippers at a child's pace, with a pause whenever they need one.

  4. 4

    Wash & style

    The itchy little hairs are brushed and rinsed away, then the hair is styled.

  5. 5

    Mirror moment

    A look in the mirror, a big smile and a new haircut to show off at home.

For parents

Tips for a smooth sail

A few small things make a big difference, especially for a first haircut or a nervous child.

Pick a happy hour

Come after a nap or a snack. A rested, fed child sits still for longer.

Bring a favourite toy

Something to hold in the chair keeps small hands busy and calm.

Let them watch first

Seeing a brother, sister or parent go first shows there's nothing to worry about.

Tell us what helps

Scared of clippers, sensitive skin or a special style? Mention it when you book on WhatsApp.

Good to know

Questions from parents

Do I need to book?

Booking is the easiest way to avoid a wait with a little one. Send us a WhatsApp with your child's name, age and the time you'd like, and we will confirm your chair.

Can I stay with my child?

Yes. Children are cut with a parent or guardian with them, and a familiar face nearby helps them relax.

My child is scared of clippers. What can you do?

Tell us when you book. We can go slowly, show the clippers first, or use scissors only for the first visits.

Is it a good place for a first haircut?

A barbershop just for children is a good place to start. Choose a calm time of day and give yourselves a little extra time for that first visit.

Do you cut girls' hair too?

Yes, we do fringe trims, tidy ends and simple cuts for girls. Ask on WhatsApp about longer or special styles.
