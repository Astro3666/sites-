Room by room

What a deep clean covers

A clear checklist for every room, so you know exactly what you are booking.

Living & bedrooms

  • Dust every surface, shelf and frame
  • Vacuum and mop all floors
  • Wipe doors, handles and light switches
  • Clean mirrors and glass tables
  • Dust fans, AC vents and skirting
  • Straighten cushions and throws

Kitchen

  • Degrease the hob and splashback
  • Wipe cabinet doors and handles
  • Clean the outside of the oven, fridge and microwave
  • Scrub and shine the sink and taps
  • Wipe counters and the dining table
  • Mop the floor, edges included

Bathrooms

  • Descale taps, shower head and glass
  • Scrub tiles and grout
  • Clean and disinfect the toilet and basin
  • Polish mirrors
  • Wipe cabinets and shelves
  • Mop and dry the floor

Windows & balcony

  • Wipe inside glass streak-free
  • Clean frames, tracks and sills
  • Dust blinds and curtain rails
  • Sweep and mop the balcony
  • Wipe the balcony door glass
  • Clear sand out of window tracks

Made for Muscat homes

Dust, sun and sea air

Homes here get dirty in their own way. We clean for the conditions you actually live with.

01

Dust gets in

Fine dust from the mountains and the desert wind settles on every shelf within days, even with the windows shut.

02

Humid sea air

Coastal humidity feeds mould in bathrooms, in corners and behind furniture.

03

Strong sun

Bright afternoon light shows every streak on the glass and every smear on a glossy floor.

04

Sand in the tracks

Window and door tracks collect sand that wears the seals and makes doors stick.

Questions

Good to know

How long does a clean take?

It depends on the size of the home and the type of clean. We give you a time estimate together with the price.

Can I book a regular visit?

Yes. Tell us which day suits you and how often, weekly or every two weeks, and we keep that slot for you.

What should I do before the team arrives?

Put away valuables and anything fragile you would rather move yourself, and tell us about surfaces that need special care, such as marble or wood.

Which areas do you cover?

We work across Muscat. Send us your area and we will confirm.
