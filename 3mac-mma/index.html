Questions

Before you come in

I have never trained. Can I join?

Yes. Most members started with zero experience. Beginners learn the fundamentals first and only spar when they and the coach agree they are ready.

What should I bring to my first class?

Comfortable sports clothes, a water bottle and a towel. If you continue, the coach will tell you which gloves, shin guards or mouthguard you need.

Do I have to fight or spar?

No. Plenty of members train for fitness and self-defence and never compete. Sparring is always optional and always controlled.

Are there classes for women and for kids?

Message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you which sessions suit you or your children and when they run.

How much is a membership?

It depends on how many sessions a week you want. Send us a WhatsApp and we will share the current packages.
