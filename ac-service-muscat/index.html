Questions

Good to know

Something else on your mind? Send us a message and ask.

How often should an AC be cleaned in Muscat?

Twice a year works for most homes: once before the summer and once after it. Rinse the filters yourself every month or two when it is dusty.

Isn't rinsing the filter enough?

The filter stops the big dust, but fine dust and moisture still build up on the coil and blower wheel behind it. That is what makes a unit smell, drip and cool slowly.

Will it make a mess in my room?

No. The unit and the area under it are covered before cleaning, and the dirty water is caught and taken away.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the type and number of units. Send us the details and we will give you a clear price before we come.
