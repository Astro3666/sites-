How it works

From the basket to the hanger

The same four steps the scene at the top acts out, done by hand and checked at every stage.

  1. 01

    Check & wash

    Pockets emptied, stains noted and treated first, colours and whites sorted, then the right wash for each fabric.

  2. 02

    Dry

    Dried at the right heat so cotton does not shrink and delicate fabric keeps its shape.

  3. 03

    Press

    Steam pressed until the creases are gone, with sharp lines where they belong.

  4. 04

    Fold or hang

    Folded neatly or hung and covered, tagged with your name and ready to collect.

Questions

Good to know

Anything else, ask at the counter.

How long does it take?

You get a collection time when you drop off. Ask about express service if you need it sooner. Add usual turnaround

Can you get stains out?

Stains are treated before washing. Tell us what caused it (oil, ink, food, henna, perfume) because that decides the treatment. Very old or set-in stains cannot always be removed fully, and you will be told before any work starts.

Is it safe for abayas with beads or lace?

Embellished abayas and delicate fabrics are dry cleaned or washed gently by hand and pressed at low heat. Point out anything special when you drop it off.

How should I bring my clothes?

In any bag. Mention stains, loose buttons or delicate pieces at the counter, and say whether you want them hung or folded.
