        Before you come in

        Do I need to book?

        Booking holds a time for you. If you want to come now, message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you if a chair is free.

        What are your opening hours?

        Saturday to Thursday from 10am to 2pm and from 3pm to 11pm. Friday from 3pm to 11pm.

        Are you open on Friday?

        Yes, from 3pm until 11pm.

        How much is a haircut?

        Prices are being added to this page. Send us a WhatsApp and we will tell you the price before you come.

        What if I am running late?

        Message us as soon as you know, so we can keep your chair or move you to the next free time.
