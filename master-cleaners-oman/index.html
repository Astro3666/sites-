    Questions

    Good to know

    How do I book a clean?

    Use the planner below or message us on Instagram with your home type, number of rooms, the service and the day you'd like. We reply with a price and a time.

    What is the difference between a regular and a deep clean?

    A regular clean keeps a home in good shape: dusting, floors, kitchen and bathrooms. A deep clean goes further: inside cupboards, behind appliances, grout, skirting boards and the spots that build up over months.

    Can I book just once, or set up regular visits?

    Both. Book a one-time clean, or choose weekly, every two weeks or monthly visits.

    Do I need to be at home during the clean?

    Not necessarily. Tell us how you'd like to arrange access when you book.
