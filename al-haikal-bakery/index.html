How it's made

From dough ball to basket

The same four steps the kitchen window at the top of this page is acting out, for every paratha that leaves the counter.

01 · Dough

A soft ball

A ball of dough, dusted with flour and ready on the board.

02 · Filling

The stuffing goes in

Rolled out and filled with potato, cauliflower, chicken or mutton.

03 · Seal & roll

Closed and rolled thin

Folded shut around the filling, then rolled out again into a wide, thin round.

04 · Tawa

Golden and flipped

Cooked on the hot tawa with a little ghee, flipped once, and handed over hot.
