When to call

Six signs your AC needs a clean

Weak airflow

Dust packs the filter and coil, so less air reaches the room.

Musty smell

Mould grows on a damp, dirty coil and blower wheel.

Water dripping

A blocked drain pan overflows down the wall.

Higher bills

A dirty unit runs longer to reach the same temperature.

Ice on the pipes

Usually low airflow or low gas. Switch the unit off and call us.

Rattling or noise

Dirt on the blower wheel throws it off balance.

Questions

Good to know

How often should I clean my AC in Oman?

We recommend a deep clean twice a year, once before summer and once after, plus a filter rinse every month or two in dusty seasons.

How long does a cleaning take?

Usually about an hour for each split unit, depending on how dirty it is and where it is mounted.

Will water or dirt get on my walls and furniture?

No. We fit a cover bag that catches the runoff and lay protection down before we start.

Do you work with offices and shops?

Yes. We clean and service units for homes, villas, offices and shops, and offer scheduled maintenance plans.
