Services

Everything your paint needs to shine again

The Omani sun, sand and brush car washes leave paint dull and covered in fine circles. We take the damage out of the clear coat, then protect what is left.

Gloss

Machine polish

A one-step polish that lifts light swirls, water spots and dullness, and brings back the depth and colour of your paint.

Correction

Paint correction

Compounding, then polishing, in stages for heavier swirls, holograms and light scratches, until the reflection is clean and sharp.

Spot repair

Scratch and mark removal

Spot work on light scratches, scuffs and bird-dropping etching. If a scratch is through the clear coat, we tell you honestly.

Clarity

Headlight restoration

Yellow, cloudy headlights wet-sanded and polished clear again, so the car looks newer and lights the road better at night.

Protection

Wax and sealant

A protective layer after polishing that keeps the gloss longer, beads the water off and makes dust easier to wash away.

How we work

Five steps to a true gloss

1Look

Inspect

We check the paint under a strong light to see the swirls, scratches and how much clear coat there is to work with.

2Clean

Wash and decontaminate

A careful wash and clay treatment take off the grit that would otherwise be ground into the paint.

3Cut

Compound

A cutting compound levels the deeper swirls and marks, one small section at a time.

4Refine

Polish

A finer polish removes the haze left by compounding and brings the paint to a wet, deep shine.

5Seal

Protect

Wax or sealant goes on by hand to protect the finish from sun and dust. Then the keys go back to you.

Questions

Good to know

How long does a polish take?

It depends on the size of the car and the condition of the paint. A one-step polish is quicker than a full correction. We give you a time when you book.

Will polishing remove every scratch?

Polishing removes swirls and light scratches in the clear coat. A scratch deep enough to catch your fingernail can usually be made much less visible, but may need paint to disappear completely. We tell you before we start.

Is polishing safe for my paint?

Polishing takes off a very thin layer of clear coat to level it. Done with care, that leaves plenty of protection. We only remove what is needed to get the result.

How do I keep the shine afterwards?

Protect it with wax or sealant, wash gently with a clean mitt, and avoid brush car washes, which put the swirls back. Ask us about protection when you book.
