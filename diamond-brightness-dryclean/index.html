Our standard

The 4 Cs of a clean garment

Jewellers grade a diamond on four Cs. We check every piece on four of our own before it leaves the counter.

Like a diamond's colour

Colour

Whites come back white, not grey or yellow. Dark abayas and navy suits keep their depth instead of fading.

Checked: whites beside a white card, darks in daylight

Like a diamond's clarity

Clarity

No stain, no tide ring and no shadow of one. Stains are treated before cleaning, never pressed in by the iron.

Checked: under bright light, front and back

Like a diamond's cut

Cut

The shape the tailor gave it. Sharp collars and cuffs, a straight dishdasha front, pleats where pleats belong.

Checked: on the hanger, collar and seams

Instead of carat

Care

The right method for each fabric: solvent, water or hand work. Embroidery, beads and zari trim protected.

Checked: the care label, before anything else

Before you drop off

How long does an order take?

It depends on the item and the season. Your ready date is written on your ticket when you drop off.

✎ Add your usual turnaround and any express option

Can every stain come out?

Most can, especially fresh ones. A stain that has been washed or ironed in may only fade. Tell us what it is and how old it is, and we will tell you honestly what to expect.

Is dry cleaning safe for embroidery and beads?

Usually, but trims vary. Point out embroidery, zari, beads, crystals or sequins at the counter so we can choose a method that protects them.

Why did my white dishdasha turn yellow in the cupboard?

Traces of sweat, perfume and oud oil oxidise over time, faster in Muscat's heat. Clean whites before storing them, and keep them in cloth rather than plastic.

What should I tell you when I drop off?

What each stain is, roughly how old it is, and whether you have already tried to remove it. Those three facts decide the treatment.
