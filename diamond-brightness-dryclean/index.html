How an order moves

From counter to hanger

Every piece gets a numbered ticket at the counter and keeps it until you collect it.

  1. 01

    Drop off & ticket

    We count your items, note the stains you point out and give you a ticket with your ready date.

  2. 02

    Check & pre-treat

    Each piece is checked against its care label, pockets are emptied and stains are treated first.

  3. 03

    Clean

    Dry cleaned or washed, whichever the fabric needs. Whites are kept apart so they stay white.

  4. 04

    Steam press

    Collars, cuffs and pleats pressed by hand. Creases out, shape back in.

  5. 05

    Bag & collect

    On a hanger, in a clear bag, with your ticket number on it. Ready to wear.

Care labels

What the little symbols mean

The tag inside every garment tells you how it wants to be cleaned. These are the ones we read every day.

Machine wash, 30 °C

The number is the highest water temperature in degrees Celsius.

Hand wash only

Cool water, gentle squeezing, no wringing. Common on kummas and fine knits.

Do not bleach

No chlorine bleach. Whites are brightened with gentler oxygen-based products instead.

Iron, medium heat

One dot is up to 110 °C, two dots 150 °C, three dots 200 °C.

Professional dry clean

Safe in the standard dry-cleaning solvents. This is our everyday dry clean.

Gentle dry clean

Mild solvent only. A line underneath means a shorter, gentler cycle.

Do not dry clean

Solvents would harm it. We wash it by hand or wet-clean it instead.

Tumble dry, low

One dot means low heat. A cross over it means hang or lay flat to dry.

Questions

Good to know

How long does an order take?

It depends on the item and how busy the season is. Your ready date is written on your ticket when you drop off.

✎ Add your usual turnaround and any express option
Can every stain come out?

Most can, especially fresh ones. Old stains that have been washed or ironed in may only fade. Tell us what the stain is and how old it is, and we will tell you honestly what to expect.

Is dry cleaning safe for embroidery and beads?

Usually yes, but trims vary. Point out embroidery, beads, crystals or sequins when you drop off, so we can choose the right method and protect them.

How should I store clothes after cleaning?

For everyday wear, the bag is fine. For long storage, take the plastic off and use a cloth cover, so the fabric can breathe in Muscat's humidity and whites do not yellow.
