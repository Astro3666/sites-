The ritual

The Turkish shave, in five steps

A shave the way it is done in the barbershops of Istanbul: slow, hot, close and finished with a splash of lemon cologne.

01

Hot towel

Steaming towels open the pores and soften the beard.

02

Lather

Warm foam is whipped in a brass bowl and brushed on.

03

Straight razor

Two careful passes, with and across the grain.

04

Cold towel & cologne

A cold towel closes the skin, then fresh lemon kolonya.

05

Finish

Balm, a quick style and you walk out feeling new.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?

No, walk-ins are always welcome. If you want a set time, especially in the evening or on weekends, send us a WhatsApp and we will hold a chair for you.

How long does a Turkish shave take?

Plan on about 30 to 40 minutes. The hot towels and two razor passes are what make it close and comfortable, so we never rush it.

Is a straight razor safe for sensitive skin?

Yes. The skin is prepared with hot towels and lather first, a new blade is used for every client and we finish with a cold towel and soothing balm. Tell your barber if you react to fragrance and we will skip the cologne.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes, kids are welcome. Quieter mornings are the best time for little ones.

Can you prepare a groom for his wedding?

Yes. Our groom package covers the cut, shave, facial and styling. Book a few days ahead so we can reserve the time, and bring the groomsmen too.
