In the chair

How a Turkish shave goes

Four steps, from hot to cold. The same ritual plays in the arch at the top of this page.

HotCold
I

Hot towel

A steaming towel rests on your face to soften the beard and open the skin.

II

Lather

Shaving soap is whipped in a bowl and brushed on in circles so every hair stands up.

III

Straight razor

Short, slow strokes with the grain, then across, with a fresh blade in the razor.

IV

Cold towel and kolonya

A cold towel closes the skin, then lemon kolonya and balm to finish.

Steps describe the traditional Turkish shave. The shop may adjust them for your skin and beard.

Questions

Before you come in

Are you open on Friday?

Yes. On Friday the shop opens at 1pm and stays open until midnight. Every other day it is 10am to midnight.

Do I need to book?

Booking holds a chair for you. If you want to come now, send a WhatsApp message and the shop will tell you if a chair is free.

How long does a Turkish shave take?

Plan for about half an hour for the full hot towel shave. Add time if you also want a haircut.

Where do I park?

The shop is on Al Ghubrah Street. Open the map link for directions; parking details will be added soon.
