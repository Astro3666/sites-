The film

A clear layer that takes the hit instead of your paint

Paint protection film is a tough, clear polyurethane skin laid over the car's own clear coat. Stones, sand blast and car park scuffs mark the film, not the paint, and the film can be replaced when it has done its job.

  • Stops chipsGravel thrown up on the highway bounces off instead of breaking through to the metal.
  • Hides light scratchesOn self-healing films, fine marks in the top coat level out with warmth from the sun.
  • Keeps the colourThe film carries UV protection, so the paint under it stays as it left the factory.

    How we work

    Four steps, in this order

    1. 01

      Wash and decontaminate

      Any grit left on the paint ends up trapped under film or coating, so the surface is cleaned down to bare paint first.

    2. 02

      Correct the paint

      Swirls and light scratches are polished out where needed. Protection seals in whatever is underneath it.

    3. 03

      Fit and protect

      Film is cut to each panel, squeegeed on with slip solution and wrapped into the edges. Coatings are levelled panel by panel.

    4. 04

      Cure and hand over

      The car rests while everything settles. We check it under the lights and explain the aftercare before you drive off.

    Answers

    Questions people ask before booking

    Will I see the film on the car?

    Gloss film is very hard to spot once fitted, and edges are wrapped where the panel allows. Matte film is also an option if you want to change the finish.

    Film or ceramic coating?

    Film stops stone chips and scrapes. Ceramic adds gloss and makes washing easy but will not stop a chip. Many owners put film on the front and coat the rest.

    Can film go on a car that is not new?

    Yes. We correct the paint first so no swirls or marks are sealed under the film, then fit it the same way as on a new car.

    How long does it take?

    It depends on the coverage, the car and the paint's condition. We give you a clear time frame when we see the car.
