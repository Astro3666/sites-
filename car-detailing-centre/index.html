Our process

Four stages to a finish that lasts

Polishing over dirt only grinds it in, and coating over swirls only seals them under glass. The order matters, so we never skip a stage.

01

Inspect

We check the paint under a strong inspection light and show you the swirls, scratches and spots before we quote.

02

Decontaminate

Foam, hand wash, iron remover and clay leave the surface truly bare, so nothing gets dragged around by the pad.

03

Correct

The paint is machine polished panel by panel, checked under the light again, and refined until it is clear and sharp.

04

Protect

Ceramic coating or sealant goes on, levelled and left to cure. Water beads and rolls straight off the finished car.

Questions

Good to know before you book

How long does correction and ceramic coating take?

It depends on the size of the car and how much correction the paint needs, and the coating needs time to cure before the car goes home. We give you a clear time frame when we inspect the car.

Will polishing remove every scratch?

Polishing removes swirls and light scratches that sit in the clear coat. A scratch you can catch with a fingernail may be too deep to remove safely, and we will tell you that before we start.

Is ceramic coating worth it in Oman's heat?

Yes. The coating adds a hard, glossy layer between the paint and the sun, dust and bird droppings, and it makes every wash quicker because dirt does not stick as easily.

How do I wash a coated car?

Use a pH-neutral shampoo, two buckets and a clean microfibre mitt, and avoid automatic brush washes. We explain the full routine when you collect the car.
