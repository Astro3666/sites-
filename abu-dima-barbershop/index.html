In the chair

How the cut goes

  1. 1

    Talk it through

    Show a photo or describe the look. Your barber checks how your hair grows and where it sits before the first cut.

  2. 2

    Shape it, row by row

    Clippers for the sides, then scissor over comb across the top so the length stays even and follows the shape of your head.

  3. 3

    Lines, then the mirror

    Clean edges at the neck, ears and beard, then a look in the mirror. Ask for any change before you get up.
